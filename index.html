Argento Assistant

A WhatsApp assistant for teams at trade shows and conferences.

Argento Assistant answers questions from a company's own team during an event: the meeting agenda, product information and internal briefing material, in English or Italian, over WhatsApp.

Who can use it

Access is by invitation. The assistant replies only to phone numbers its operator has registered for a given event. Messages from anyone else receive a short notice and are not processed.

Your data

  • Messages are used only to answer the person who sent them and to share meeting notes with that person's own team.
  • We do not send marketing messages and do not sell or share data with third parties.
  • Conversations are kept to improve the assistant for the team. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

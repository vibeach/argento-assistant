Privacy policy

This policy explains how Argento Assistant handles information sent to it over WhatsApp. Last updated 27 September 2026 (retention updated).

What we collect

Your WhatsApp phone number, your display name, and the messages you send to the assistant.

How we use it

To answer your questions; to share what you report (meeting outcomes, contacts, business cards and notes) with your own team through the assistant; and to improve the assistant over time. We do not use it for marketing and we do not send promotional messages.

Who we share it with

No one outside your team. Messages are delivered through WhatsApp (Meta) and Twilio, which process them as service providers. We do not sell data.

How long we keep it

Conversations and the contact information shared with the assistant are kept for as long as the team uses the service, so it can keep improving. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or stop using the service, by writing to mia.argento08@gmail.com.
